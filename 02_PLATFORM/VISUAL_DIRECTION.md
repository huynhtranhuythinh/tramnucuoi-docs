# VISUAL DIRECTION --- TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI

## Main Direction

Photorealistic documentary editorial.

The website should feel like: - a field journal - an impact report - a
premium cultural publication

## Preferred Images

-   real Vietnamese communities
-   bamboo landscapes
-   local craftsmanship
-   people working together
-   conversations
-   rural environments
-   nature connection

## Avoid

-   illustration as primary image
-   cartoon
-   watercolor
-   generic stock photo
-   artificial corporate imagery

## Design Language

Use: - warm ivory - deep navy - sun yellow - natural green - earthy
tones

Visual elements: - organic lines - subtle textures - editorial spacing -
storytelling layouts

## Motion

Use subtle: - image reveal - scroll transition - gentle movement

Avoid excessive animation.
