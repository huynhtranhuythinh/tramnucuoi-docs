# CONTENT GUIDELINE --- TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI

## Writing Philosophy

Content should tell a living story, not advertise a project.

The narrative should connect: - people - place - nature - culture -
livelihood - future possibility

## Vietnamese Tone

-   chân thành
-   sâu sắc
-   gần gũi
-   giàu tính quan sát

Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

## English Tone

English should be suitable for: - international partners - foundations -
social impact readers

Style: - professional - thoughtful - clear - human

## Journal Articles

Each field note should include: - title - summary - story - reflection -
image - category - tags

## Content Safety

Do not create false: - impact numbers - partnerships - success claims -
community results
