# TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI --- PROJECT RULES

## Product Vision

TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI is a Living Ecosystem Storytelling Platform, not a
traditional company website.

The website should communicate: - people - nature - local value -
sustainable livelihood - community connection - ecosystem thinking

## Core Positioning

Avoid: - generic NGO website - corporate landing page - tourism
brochure - fundraising-only communication

Build as: - living story - impact journal - ecosystem narrative -
international-facing credibility platform

## Language

The product is bilingual: - Vietnamese (primary) - English
(international)

All important public content must support VN/EN editing.

Never invent: - partners - achievements - statistics - legal information

## Visual

Brand feeling: - Warm - Human - Trustworthy - Sustainable - Contemporary
Vietnamese

Image rule: PHOTOREALISTIC DOCUMENTARY STYLE

AI visuals are prototypes only and should look like real photography.
