## 4. FINAL EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

### 4.1 Public Story World

Canonical public story flow:

Home → About → Ecosystem → Project → Journey → Community

The experience is story-first and documentary/editorial. System state supports the story rather than becoming the primary visual language.

### 4.2 Journey World

Canonical Journey lifecycle:

**BEFORE**

Discover → trust → understand → register → confirmation → prepare.

**DURING**

Participate → experience → documentary context.

**AFTER**

Attendance truth → Memory → Reflection → Contribution → relationship continuity.

One Journey is experienced as one lifecycle rather than disconnected modules.

### 4.3 My TNC

My TNC is a private personal relationship archive, not a SaaS dashboard and not a member-directory product.

Canonical idea:

**VI:** `Những lần tôi và Trạm đã gặp nhau.`

**EN:** `The times Trạm and I have met.`

Only truthful personal sections appear. Legitimate empty states are accepted as valid product states.

### 4.4 Living Community

Public Community communicates shared presence through real Journeys, documentary moments, moderated/publication-approved stories and sourced relationships.

Canonical idea:

**VI:** `Cộng đồng hiện ra qua những lần chúng ta cùng đi.`

**EN:** `Community becomes visible in the times we journey together.`

The product does not introduce a public member directory, social feed, leaderboard, points, badges or vanity activity counters.

### 4.5 Post-Journey Continuity

Memory, Reflection, Contribution and relationship continuity remain evidence-aware.

Presentation-side evidence-backed Memory requires all applicable canonical truth conditions, including verified attended state, positive observed attended party size and Memory eligibility.

Reflection authoring remains gated by completed Journey state plus evidence-backed Memory. Private Reflection submission remains distinct from public publication.
