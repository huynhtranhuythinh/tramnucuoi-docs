## 1. PURPOSE

This canon closes the Social Home Product / UX Rebase for TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI.

The redesign does not turn TNC into a generic social network. It preserves the governing product principle:

> **TRẠM NỤ CƯỜI is a Journey-Based Social Network.**

> **Journey — not Post and not Person — is the primary social object.**

The approved experience uses familiar social interaction patterns while ensuring that social meaning, authorship, relationships and memory all originate from real Journey participation.

---

## 2. CANONICAL PRODUCT THESIS

TNC is not:

- a Facebook clone;
- an event portal with comments;
- a generic community wall;
- a follower-first social network;
- a feed optimized for engagement volume.

TNC is:

> **A social world formed around real Journeys.**

Canonical social loop:

**Discover Journey**  
→ **Apply**  
→ **Approved**  
→ **Become Companions**  
→ **Prepare Together**  
→ **Experience Together**  
→ **Share Moments**  
→ **React / Comment / Reply**  
→ **Return**  
→ **Remember Together**  
→ **Relationship Persists**  
→ **Meet Again in Another Journey**

---

## 3. APPROVED SOCIAL HOME DIRECTION

Approved direction:

> **Hybrid TNC Social World**

Interaction familiarity follows conventions users already understand from mainstream social products.

Meaning remains TNC-specific:

> **Journey → People → Shared Experience → Story → Relationship → Memory → Next Journey**

People are the emotional surface. Journey is the meaning layer.

Home is not a collection of website sections. It is a continuous living social stream.

---

## 4. SOCIAL HOME MENTAL MODEL

Home answers four questions:

1. What is happening now?
2. Who is involved?
3. What is relevant to me?
4. Where can I participate next?

Canonical Home layers:

1. **Identity / Journey-aware composer**
2. **Journey Pulse**
3. **Personalized Living Feed**
4. **Relationship signals**
5. **Journey signals**
6. **Stories / Moments**
7. **Memory / Continuity**
8. **Next Journey**

Home must not become a dashboard or a collection of disconnected modules.

---

## 5. FEED GRAMMAR V1

Canonical feed objects:

1. **People Story**
2. **Journey Signal**
3. **Live Moment**
4. **Volunteer Need**
5. **Relationship Signal**
6. **Reflection**
7. **Memory**
8. **Reunion / Next Journey**

Feed ranking must prioritize:

1. Journey relevance;
2. Companion/shared-experience relevance;
3. lifecycle timing;
4. social significance.

Reaction count must not become the primary ranking engine.

There is no engagement-farming objective.

---

## 6. JOURNEY SOCIAL CARD SYSTEM

Canonical Journey lifecycle variants:

1. **Upcoming**
2. **Preparation**
3. **Live**
4. **Returned**
5. **Memory**

Canonical presentation variants:

- Full
- Compact
- Relationship-first

Every Journey card must include at least one human signal such as:

- companion avatars;
- participant count;
- shared Journey context;
- human photography;
- a story or quote.

A card containing only title, date, location and CTA is an Event Card and is not sufficient for TNC Social UX.

---

## 7. JOURNEY IS THE SOCIAL CONTAINER

Locked rule:

> **Journey is not merely a topic or tag. Journey is the social container.**

There is no generic global wall.

There is no original social post without a Journey context.

Canonical rules:

> **No Journey, no original social post.**

> **Participation creates authorship; shared experience creates relationship.**

Only users with verified/approved participation or an authorized Journey role may create original Journey content.

Users outside a Journey may interact according to visibility and policy but may not create original posts inside that Journey.

---

## 8. JOURNEY SOCIAL ROOM

Journey Social Room is the primary place where TNC social life happens.

Canonical lifecycle experience modes:

1. **Forming** — We are about to travel together.
2. **Preparing** — We are preparing together.
3. **Experiencing** — We are here together.
4. **Remembering** — We were here together.

Approved primary tabs:

- **Cùng nhau**
- **Đồng hành**
- **Khoảnh khắc**

Journey practical information is a secondary surface and must not dominate the social room.

The Social Room remains available after Journey completion and transitions into Memory Mode.

---

## 9. COMPANION RELATIONSHIP MODEL

TNC does not use Friend Request as the primary relationship mechanism.

Canonical states:

1. **Sắp đồng hành**
2. **Đang đồng hành**
3. **Đã từng đồng hành**
4. **Đã cùng nhau qua X Hành Trình**
5. **Cùng đội** as contextual relationship

A shared Journey before it happens does not prove a past shared real-world experience.

A completed shared-experience relationship must respect evidence/truth boundaries.

Interaction volume alone never creates a Companion relationship.

Profile and Home should expose meaningful Journey context instead of follower vanity metrics.

---

## 10. CONTENT CREATION MODEL

User-facing content modes:

- **Khoảnh khắc**
- **Câu chuyện**
- **Nhìn lại**
- **Cập nhật từ Hành Trình** for authorized organizer roles

One simple composer adapts by lifecycle.

Canonical prompts:

- Forming: **Chào những người bạn sắp đồng hành…**
- Preparing: **Chia sẻ với những người đồng hành…**
- Experiencing: **Chia sẻ khoảnh khắc…**
- Remembering: **Bạn còn nhớ điều gì từ Hành Trình này?**

During an active Journey, creation is media-first / camera-first.

Do not require CMS-like metadata such as title, taxonomy, SEO fields or tags for normal social creation.

All original content permanently retains:

> **Author + Journey + Time**

---

## 11. VISIBILITY & PUBLICATION

Canonical separation:

> **Journey-room visibility is not the same as public publication.**

Default Journey social content should remain scoped to the Journey / companions unless a broader audience is explicitly allowed.

Possible user-facing visibility levels:

- Người đồng hành
- Cộng đồng TNC
- Công khai

Only options permitted by policy/consent may be shown.

Public publication remains a separate consent/publication decision from private operational truth.

---

## 12. INTERACTION MODEL V1

Core interactions:

- **Cảm xúc**
- **Bình luận**
- **Trả lời**
- **Theo dõi Hành Trình**
- **Quan tâm**
- **Tham gia**
- **Notification**
- **Share link** only where permitted

Canonical rules:

- interaction may be broader than authorship;
- Follow Journey does not create Companion status;
- reactions acknowledge;
- comments create conversation;
- shared Journey creates relationship;
- notifications surface meaning, not activity volume;
- interaction never replaces real-world Journey truth.

Replies are visually nested one level.

There is no reaction leaderboard, trending leaderboard, follower race or streak mechanic.

---

## 13. USER ACCESS STATES

Canonical social states:

### Anonymous — Observe
Can view permitted public social content.

### Member — Interact + Discover
Can react/comment/follow/apply according to policy, but has no original Journey authorship without participation.

### Applicant — Await + Stay Connected
Application state is visible and prioritized, but no Companion-only content or authorship is granted.

### Companion — Belong + Create + Experience + Remember
Approved participant enters the full Journey social experience.

Important:

> **Companion is a Person↔Journey relationship, not a global account role.**

A person may simultaneously be Companion in Journey A, Applicant in Journey B and Member relative to Journey C.

---

## 14. APPROVAL IS THE SOCIAL ACTIVATION EVENT

Creating an account is not the primary TNC social activation event.

Canonical threshold:

> **Application Approved → Become Companion**

Approval UX should communicate belonging, not merely transaction success.

Example meaning:

> **Bạn đã trở thành người đồng hành của Hành Trình này.**

This unlocks Journey authorship and the full Social Room permitted by policy.

## 15. MOBILE NAVIGATION — CANONICAL

Mobile is the canonical social experience.

Logged-in bottom navigation:

- **Trang chủ**
- **Hành Trình**
- **＋**
- **Cộng đồng**
- **Tôi**

Anonymous may use a reduced navigation without the create affordance.

Journey Room:

- **Cùng nhau**
- **Đồng hành**
- **Khoảnh khắc**

No global Chat tab in V1.

No Friends tab.

No generic global composer.

No admin/operations module inside social bottom navigation.

---

## 16. GLASS NAVIGATION SYSTEM — LOCKED

All mobile social surfaces must use one unified navigation language.

Canonical behavior:

- floating translucent / glass bottom navigation;
- visually layered above content rather than hard-docked to the viewport edge;
- matching translucent/layered header language;
- header and bottom navigation automatically hide while the user scrolls forward/down into content;
- header and bottom navigation reappear smoothly when the user scrolls back/up;
- immersive surfaces such as Camera or full-screen Media Viewer may temporarily hide global navigation;
- iconography, spacing, blur, transparency, radius, height and motion behavior must remain consistent across all Social surfaces.

This applies to:

- Social Home;
- Journey;
- Journey Room;
- Community;
- Profile;
- Notifications;
- Search;
- Post/Moment Detail;
- comments;
- social discovery surfaces.

- empty state;

## 24. SOCIAL DESIGN LANGUAGE V1

Canonical visual characteristics:

- warm off-white / neutral canvas;
- white / translucent layered surfaces;
- restrained TNC yellow as primary accent;
- strong dark text hierarchy;
- face-led real photography;
- minimal decorative chrome;
- soft depth;
- generous but compact mobile spacing;
- Journey state as contextual accent, not dashboard status chips;
- circular avatars;
- familiar social interaction icons;
- consistent Glass Navigation System.

Do not use:

- heavy gradients as marketing decoration;
- enterprise dashboard visual language;
- NGO portal visual language;
- excessive pills/chips;
- dense technical labels;
- arbitrary card variations.

## 26. TRUTH & PRIVACY BOUNDARIES

This UX canon does not override:

- Journey operational truth;
- participant approval truth;
- attendance evidence;
- Shared Experience verification;
- privacy boundaries;
- publication consent;
- moderation/safety controls;
- RLS/security constraints.

UI must never manufacture a Companion relationship or shared experience that the truth layer does not support.

---

## 27. IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY

This document is a product/UX canon.

It does **not** by itself authorize:

- destructive schema rewrites;
- weakening privacy/RLS;
- replacing verified Journey/participant truth;
- merging public and private publication scopes;
- enabling unsupported social claims.

Implementation must reuse existing canonical truth where compatible and add only the minimum required capability.

---

## 28. FINAL UX ACCEPTANCE TEST

A successful implementation must pass these product questions:

### Five-second Home test
A Companion opening TNC should understand within seconds:

- I am in TNC.
- A Journey relevant to me is active or approaching.
- My companions are here.
- Something is happening.
- I can participate or share from the Journey.

### Journey Room test
The room must feel different before, during and after the Journey without becoming three unrelated products.

### Authenticity test
Every original social post must have a valid Journey source.

### Relationship test
Companion context must come from Journey truth, not generic friend/follow mechanics.

### Familiarity test
Reaction, comments, media, navigation and scrolling should be understandable without a tutorial.

### TNC identity test
Even with familiar interaction conventions, the experience must not be mistaken for a generic Facebook clone.
