# Community Governance

## Purpose

Define how people participate safely and meaningfully inside TNC Community.

---

# Publishing Rules

Not every participant can publish official content.

## Official Content

Created by:

- TNC Team
- Authorized Contributors

## Participant Content

May require review before public publishing.

---

# Role Trust Model

Users gain access through:

- participation
- contribution
- trust
- administrator approval

---

# Governance Principles

1. Protect community dignity.
2. Respect local people and places.
3. Avoid unsupported impact claims.
4. Preserve evidence accuracy.
5. Maintain a safe community environment.

---

# Admin Responsibilities

Admin manages:

- roles
- permissions
- content approval
- community safety
- documentation standards
